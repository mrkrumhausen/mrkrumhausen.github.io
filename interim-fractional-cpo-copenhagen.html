Frequently asked

What is the difference between an interim CPO and a fractional CPO?
An interim CPO works full time for a fixed period, usually 3 to 12 months, to fill a product leadership gap until a permanent hire starts. A fractional CPO works part time and ongoing, usually 1 to 3 days a week, without the cost of a full-time executive.

When should a company hire one?
When the CPO has left and you need cover during the search, when you're too small for a full-time CPO but need senior product leadership, or when product, engineering and sales aren't pulling in the same direction.

Interim and fractional CPO Work Principles

Sebastian Krumhausen

Interim and fractional Chief Product Officer in Copenhagen.

I'm a product leader based in Copenhagen. I step in as interim CPO (full time, for a few months) or fractional CPO (a few days a week, ongoing) when a company needs senior product leadership and doesn't have it yet, or has just lost it.

What I help with

  • Product strategy and roadmap that the whole leadership team can say out loud
  • Building, coaching or replacing product managers and product teams
  • Bridging product, engineering, design and commercial teams
  • Bringing AI into the product and the way the team works
  • Holding the seat while you hire a permanent CPO, then handing over cleanly

Background

Co-founder of Mesh Firm, a product management consultancy, and of Værksted, which builds AI-native products and helps other companies do the same. Former Head of Product at Sure, which launched as the number one Messenger bot on Product Hunt. Earlier work includes concept development for LEGO Life, a design system at TELUS Digital, and product work for Unwire and KAYAK. Master's degree from the Technical University of Denmark, with studies at University College London. More on the work page.
